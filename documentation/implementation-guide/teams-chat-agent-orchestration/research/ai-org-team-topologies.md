## Recommended Framework: **Team Topologies**

The research consistently converges on **Team Topologies** (Skelton & Pais) as the modern, purpose-built organizational framework for software-producing companies. It is an evolution of the earlier **Spotify Model** (squads/tribes/chapters/guilds) and explicitly designed to optimize three things:

1. **Flow of value** — how requirements reach the teams that build software
2. **Cognitive load** — how much each team needs to know/do
3. **Coordination patterns** — how teams communicate about changes, dependencies, and concerns

---

### Four Team Types with Defined Roles & Responsibilities

| Team Topology                   | Role                       | Primary Responsibility                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stream-Aligned Teams**        | Frontline delivery         | Own end-to-end value streams; deliver features directly to users; closest to business requirements                                                    |
| **Platform Teams**              | Internal product providers | Deliver self-service internal platforms that enable Stream-Aligned teams to deliver software rapidly and safely                                       |
| **Enabling Teams**              | Force multipliers          | Help other teams build capability through facilitation, mentoring, and teaching (e.g., DevOps coaches, architecture guilds)                           |
| **Complicated Subsystem Teams** | Specialist owners          | Maintain domains requiring deep, specialized knowledge (e.g., security crypto, kernel-level systems) that don't fit neatly into a single value stream |

### Communication Pathways

The research found:

- **Organizational structure directly dictates communication pathways, cognitive load, and overall velocity of software delivery** (Source: developers.dev guide)
- **Stream-Aligned teams** receive requirements directly from business/product stakeholders — shortest path from need to delivery
- **Cross-functional PODs** owning end-to-end value streams minimize communication bottlenecks by co-locating the skills needed to deliver
- **Platform teams** serve downstream Stream-Aligned teams via self-service (reducing coordination overhead)
- **Clear communication flows across all organizational levels** reduce confusion and facilitate smoother decision-making regarding software requirements and changes (Source: PrometAI Blueprint)

---

### Ethics & Governance Integration

- **Ethical decision-making requires both individual intention and collective organizational support** to maintain a compliance culture (Source: Compliance Culture Blog)
- **Ethical and compliance safeguards must be designed into software architecture from the initial discovery phase** rather than added as post-build audits (Source: RaftLabs)
- **Platform teams** are ideally positioned to bake ethical/compliance guardrails into the self-service platform, making the "default path" the safe path
- **Continuous monitoring of software supply chains and dependency auditing** upholds governance standards (Source: Alibaba Cloud)
